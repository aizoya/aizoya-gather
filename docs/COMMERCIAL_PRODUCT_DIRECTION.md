# Gather commercial product direction — October 29, 2026

Founder direction: commercialize the work. Hypothesis: families, couples and travel companions return for a low-pressure shared decision game. No customer demand, revenue, paid conversion or retention has been verified.

## Product job
Invite → bring tastes → discover → vote separately → reveal agreement → save a planning date.

Keep AIZOYA branding. No forced timers, public unpaid lists, charges to children or automatic bookings. A vote is not payment authorization. Qloo ranks describe retrieved cultural associations; explicit participants' votes establish acceptance.

## Current private candidate
Two to four cultural interests, four candidates where available, live Qloo search/Insights, bounded recovery and veto, dates, invitation links, separate browser-seat credentials, periodic shared state, ballot reveal after all seats submit, all-pass outcome, and calendar export. Rooms require existing Site access while the audience is private. Rooms expire for access two days after the planning date; retained records have no automatic deletion yet. Calendar exports are planning reminders, not confirmed availability or reservations.

Room creation refreshes candidates through Qloo server-side, so the final invitation can differ from the previous shortlist. Polling is serialized and stops when the page is hidden. Ballots lock atomically at reveal. Individual ballots are not shown to other participants; shared outcomes can still imply preferences. An invitation link grants the ability to fill an available seat. No participant names, emails, dates, votes, or room titles are sent to Qloo.

## Blind review
Two independent AI reviewers received Qloo capabilities and deadline, without current Gather name, design, screenshots, or source. Both independently recommended a group-decision experience rather than a general concierge. Their proposals used names Common Ground and Everyone's In; those names were not adopted. Reviewers are AI, not human customer or judge validation. Later technical review was explicitly non-blind and inspected source. Initial MODIFY findings were addressed; recheck returned GO for controlled private testing, not commercial readiness.

Interview couples/families and hospitality hosts separately. Candidate business models: free rooms plus paid saved-group features, or a hotel/host edition. Pricing and unit economics remain unknown. Verify Qloo commercial permissions/costs and retention rules before charging.
